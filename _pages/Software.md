# Software &amp; Datasets

<h2 class="sw-theme first">Imaging Through Aberrations</h2>
<div class="sw-list">

    <p>Code and data demonstrating neural wavefront shaping for imaging through static and dynamic scattering media.</p>

  </div>
  <div class="sw-item">

    <p>Code for guidestar-free, sensorless closed-loop adaptive optics using asymmetric apertures and machine learning.</p>
    <div class="sw-links"><a href="https://github.com/WeiyunJiang/guidestar-free-ao" target="_blank" rel="noopener">code</a></div>
  </div>
  <div class="sw-item">

    <p>Code for learning wavefront modulations that improve imaging through scattering media.</p>
    <div class="sw-links"><a href="https://github.com/mingyangx/wavemo" target="_blank" rel="noopener">code</a></div>
  </div>
  <div class="sw-item">

    <p>Code for single-step latent-diffusion underwater image restoration, plus the physics-based synthetic underwater image-generation pipeline.</p>
    <div class="sw-links"><a href="https://github.com/kongdai123/SLURPP" target="_blank" rel="noopener">code</a></div>
  </div>

    <p>Code for a physics-based framework that fuses optical and imaging-sonar measurements for underwater 3D surface reconstruction.</p>
    <div class="sw-links"><a href="https://github.com/kzhang2/aoneus" target="_blank" rel="noopener">code</a></div>
  </div>
  <div class="sw-item">
    <h3><a href="https://rice.app.box.com/v/TransmissionMatrices" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Transmission Matrix Dataset</a></h3>
