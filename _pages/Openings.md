# Open Positions

The **Intelligent Sensing Lab** is actively recruiting and welcomes
applicants from electrical engineering, computer science, applied math, optics, physics,
bio-engineering, and related fields.
</div>

<div class="position-card" markdown="1">
## Postdocs &amp; Research Scientists <span class="tag">Recruiting</span>

The lab has several open positions for postdoctoral researchers and research scientists in **computational
imaging, applied optics, and machine learning**. Ideal candidates have a strong publication record and hands-on
experience building or working with imaging or sensing hardware.

To apply, email **metzler [at] umd.edu** with:

- Your CV and a link to Google Scholar
- A short description of your research interests and how they connect to the lab
- Names of two or more references
</div>

<div class="position-card" markdown="1">
## PhD Students

Prospective PhD students should apply to the UMD [Computer Science](https://www.cs.umd.edu/grad/apply)
or [Electrical &amp; Computer Engineering](https://ece.umd.edu/graduate/prospective-students/admissions) PhD
program and **mention Prof. Metzler by name in their research statement**. We are especially
interested in students who want to apply machine learning to real-world optical and RF
hardware.
</div>

<div class="position-card" markdown="1">
## Undergraduate Researchers

We regularly host UMD undergraduates interested in hands-on research in imaging, machine
learning, and sensing. This is a great path toward publications and graduate school.
Students interested in joining the lab are asked to first take **CMSC498D**.
</div>
